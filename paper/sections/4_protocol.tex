\section{Experimental Protocol}
\paragraph{Data and training.}
CIFAR-10 contains 50,000 training and 10,000 test images~\cite{krizhevsky2009learning}. A fixed permutation with seed 20261107 partitions the training images into 45,000 training and 5,000 validation examples. Inputs are scaled to $[0,1]$ and horizontally flipped during training with probability 0.5. We use Adam with learning rate $10^{-4}$, batch size 128, and one training seed, 42. The optimized distortion is mean squared error (MSE) on the author's 0--255 scale:
\begin{equation}
 \mathcal L=\frac{255^2}{3BHW}\sum_{b=1}^{B}
 \|x_b-\operatorname{clip}(\hat x_b,0,1)\|_2^2,
\end{equation}
where $B$ is batch size and $H=W=32$. All modules are constructed at initialization. The first 2,000 updates use the non-adaptive forward path at 7 dB, bypassing the modulation branch. We then activate that branch in the same model instance and sample SNR uniformly from $\{1,4,7,10,13\}$ dB. Backbone weights continue from pretraining; previously bypassed modules retain their seeded constructor initialization until this transition. No new parameters are created at the boundary, where Adam is reset. Training segments preserve optimizer, sampler, and random-number-generator states.

Every 500 updates, the adaptive model is evaluated at 1, 7, and 13 dB using noise seed 9917 plus $100s$. Selection minimizes the arithmetic mean of the three MSEs, retaining the earlier checkpoint on ties. Among 41 scheduled evaluations, the selected checkpoint occurs at 21,500 total updates. Cutoff-only validations are excluded. All current deployment variants use this checkpoint. Computation uses 32-bit floating point (FP32), PyTorch 2.9.1 with CUDA 12.8, and an RTX 5070 Laptop GPU.

\paragraph{Validation before final testing.}
A historical pilot uses the 8,000-update checkpoint, 1,000 validation images, 13 fixed SNRs, and three paired channel-noise realizations. Four pilot points (2.25, 5.25, 8.25, and 11.25 dB) are off the 13-node table's knots. It compares 3-, 5-, 9-, and 13-node linear tables, a 5-node nearest-neighbor table, and a control that computes each original SNR MLP once per batch and broadcasts its output. The primary 13-node design is fixed after reviewing this pilot.

On the 21,500-update checkpoint, P002 compares the original model with 5- and 13-node linear tables using 1,000 validation images at 32 SNRs. These include every half-decibel point from 1 to 13 dB and seven additional fractional stress points. P003 compares the original and primary 13-node deployment on all 5,000 validation images at the eight SNRs subsequently used for testing. Each validation uses three paired noise realizations. Admission requires maximum signed PSNR loss at most 0.1 dB, at least 25\% reduction in serialized bytes, and a successful save-and-reload check.

Both validations pass before the checkpoint, deployment files, and P004 test protocol are frozen. P004 evaluates all 10,000 official test images at $\{1,1.5,2.375,3,7,12,12.5,13\}$ dB using three new paired noise realizations. Deployment validations P002 and P003 use noise seed base 51001; P004 uses 91001. Testing makes no further selection of knots or model parameters. Its reload check uses a deterministic synthetic ramp, separate from the official test images.

\paragraph{Metrics and conditional uncertainty.}
For $N$ distinct images at each SNR, we clip reconstructed pixels to $[0,1]$ and compute an MSE $e_{i,r}$ over the 3,072 scalar RGB channel values of image $i$ in noise realization $r$. Reported PSNR is
\begin{equation}
 P=-10\log_{10}\!\left(\frac{1}{3N}\sum_{r=1}^{3}\sum_{i=1}^{N} e_{i,r}\right).
\end{equation}
This is PSNR from pooled MSE, rather than the mean of per-image PSNRs. Signed loss is $\Delta=P_{\rm original}-P_{\rm table}$, so a negative value denotes a small improvement for the table.

For P002--P004, at each SNR, 2,000 paired bootstrap draws resample original batch IDs jointly across both methods and all three noise realizations. Each draw pools the recorded error sums and image counts, correctly weighting the shorter last batch. The full validation has 40 batch clusters (last batch: 8 images), and testing has 79 (last batch: 16). The 2.5th and 97.5th percentiles form pointwise descriptive intervals conditional on the checkpoint, fixed batch members, and recorded noises. The three noise realizations are repetitions of the channel simulation, rather than independent training seeds.

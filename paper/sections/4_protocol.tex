\section{Experimental Protocol}
\paragraph{Data and training.}
CIFAR-10 contains 50,000 training and 10,000 test images~\cite{krizhevsky2009learning}. A fixed permutation with seed 20261107 partitions the training images into 45,000 training and 5,000 validation examples. Inputs are scaled to $[0,1]$. We use Adam with learning rate $10^{-4}$, batch size 128, and one training seed, 42. Training begins with 2,000 updates of the author's non-adaptive variant at 7 dB, followed by the adaptive model with uniformly sampled SNRs from $\{1,4,7,10,13\}$ dB. Adam is reset at this stage boundary. Training segments preserve optimizer, sampler, and random-number-generator states.

Every 500 updates, the adaptive model is evaluated at 1, 7, and 13 dB. Selection minimizes the arithmetic mean of the three MSEs, retaining the earlier checkpoint on ties. Among 41 eligible evaluations, the selected checkpoint occurs at 21,500 total updates. The training process later pauses at update 22,645; a validation performed only because of a cutoff is excluded from selection. All current deployment variants use the selected checkpoint. Computation uses FP32, PyTorch 2.9.1 with CUDA 12.8, and an RTX 5070 Laptop GPU.

\paragraph{Validation before final testing.}
A historical pilot uses the 8,000-update checkpoint, 1,000 validation images, 13 fixed SNRs, and three paired channel-noise realizations. It compares 3-, 5-, 9-, and 13-node linear tables, a 5-node nearest-neighbor table, and a control that computes each original SNR MLP once per batch and broadcasts its output. The primary 13-node design is fixed after reviewing this pilot.

On the 21,500-update checkpoint, P002 compares the original model with 5- and 13-node linear tables using 1,000 validation images at 32 SNRs. These include every half-decibel point from 1 to 13 dB and seven additional fractional stress points. P003 compares the original and primary 13-node deployment on all 5,000 validation images at the eight SNRs subsequently used for testing. Each validation uses three paired noise realizations. Admission requires maximum signed PSNR loss at most 0.1 dB, at least 25\% reduction in serialized bytes, and a successful serialization witness.

Both validations pass before the checkpoint, deployment files, and P004 test protocol are frozen. P004 evaluates all 10,000 official test images at $\{1,1.5,2.375,3,7,12,12.5,13\}$ dB using three new paired noise realizations. Validation uses noise seed base 51001 and testing uses 91001. Testing makes no further selection of knots or model parameters. Its serialization witness uses a deterministic synthetic ramp, keeping that check separate from the official test images.

\paragraph{Metrics and conditional uncertainty.}
At each SNR, we clip reconstructed pixels to $[0,1]$ and compute an MSE $e_{i,r}$ over the 3,072 scalar RGB channel values of image $i$ in noise realization $r$. Reported PSNR is
\begin{equation}
 P=-10\log_{10}\!\left(\frac{1}{3N}\sum_{r=1}^{3}\sum_{i=1}^{N} e_{i,r}\right).
\end{equation}
This is PSNR from pooled MSE, rather than the mean of per-image PSNRs. Signed loss is $\Delta=P_{\rm original}-P_{\rm table}$, so a negative value denotes a small improvement for the table.

For P002--P004, at each SNR, 2,000 paired bootstrap draws resample original batch IDs jointly across both methods and all three noise realizations. Each draw pools the recorded error sums and image counts, correctly weighting the shorter last batch. The full validation has 40 batch clusters (last batch: 8 images), and testing has 79 (last batch: 16). The 2.5th and 97.5th percentiles form pointwise descriptive intervals conditional on the checkpoint, fixed batch members, and recorded noises. The three noise realizations are repetitions of the channel simulation, rather than independent training seeds.

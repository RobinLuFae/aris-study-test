\section{Related Work}
\paragraph{Learned wireless image transmission.}
DeepJSCC learns a direct mapping between image pixels and complex channel inputs~\cite{bourtsoulatze2019deep}. By optimizing reconstruction through a simulated channel, it couples source representation with resilience to transmission noise. Attention-based adaptive JSCC incorporates channel quality into feature processing~\cite{xu2022adaptive}. WITT introduces a transformer architecture and SNR-dependent feature modulation for wireless image transmission~\cite{yang2023witt}. SwinJSCC further develops Swin-transformer-based JSCC with channel and rate adaptation~\cite{yang2025swinjscc}. These studies motivate conditioning the encoder and decoder on communication constraints. Our experiment uses the original WITT components to isolate the storage cost of scalar SNR conditioning at a fixed channel-use ratio.

\paragraph{Lookup tables for neural deployment.}
Evaluating a learned function in advance and interpolating stored outputs is an established deployment strategy. Jo and Kim transfer learned image mappings into lookup tables for practical single-image super-resolution~\cite{jo2021practical}. The dimensionality of the table input is central to its storage cost: a multidimensional image neighborhood and a single channel-quality scalar lead to different table designs. Here, the tabulated input is only SNR. The table stores outputs of WITT's trained conditioning networks, while image-dependent feature transformations remain in place. The empirical contribution is the measured file-size and reconstruction tradeoff for this intervention, together with a test protocol that checks interpolation rather than only stored knots.

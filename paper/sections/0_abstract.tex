\begin{abstract}
We reduce the deployment storage of the wireless image transmission transformer (WITT) by tabulating the networks that condition it on signal-to-noise ratio (SNR). Fourteen scalar-input networks account for 4,150,272 learned parameters in our configuration. We replace them after training with 13-node FP32 tables and linear interpolation, retaining the image-dependent computation and channel-use ratio. For one locally trained checkpoint on CIFAR-10 with an additive white Gaussian noise (AWGN) channel, the complete serialized deployment file decreases from 54,987,545 to 38,644,489 bytes, a 29.72\% reduction. Validation determines the table choice before a frozen evaluation on 10,000 test images, eight predeclared SNRs, and three paired noise realizations. The maximum signed decrease in peak signal-to-noise ratio (PSNR) is 0.000186 dB. Five test conditions are stored knots and three exercise interpolation. Dense validation and a nearest-neighbor comparison characterize the storage--reconstruction tradeoff.
\end{abstract}

# Citation audit of `jo2021practical`

**EXISTENCE — YES.** CVF lists the paper in the CVPR 2021 proceedings and hosts the full paper. [CVF proceedings entry](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2021/html/Jo_Practical_Single-Image_Super-Resolution_Using_Look-Up_Table_CVPR_2021_paper.html) · [CVF full paper](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2021/papers/Jo_Practical_Single-Image_Super-Resolution_Using_Look-Up_Table_CVPR_2021_paper.pdf)

**METADATA — correct.** The authors, in order, are Younghyun Jo and Seon Joo Kim. The title is *Practical Single-Image Super-Resolution Using Look-Up Table*. It appeared at the 2021 IEEE/CVF Conference on Computer Vision and Pattern Recognition (CVPR), June 2021, pp. 691–700. The authors’ institution lists IEEE Computer Society as publisher and records DOI `10.1109/CVPR46437.2021.00075`. The submitted DOI differs only in capitalization. [CVF proceedings entry](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2021/html/Jo_Practical_Single-Image_Super-Resolution_Using_Look-Up_Table_CVPR_2021_paper.html) · [Yonsei University record](https://yonsei.elsevierpure.com/en/publications/practical-single-image-super-resolution-using-look-up-table/)

**USE 1 — SUPPORTS.** In `sections/2_related_work.tex`, the cited sentence attributes the transfer of learned image mappings into lookup tables for single-image super-resolution to Jo and Kim. Section 3.2 describes computing a trained network’s high-resolution outputs in advance and storing them in a table indexed by low-resolution pixel values. Section 3.3 describes interpolation between stored samples at inference. Their four-pixel example has 17 samples along each input axis, hence 17⁴ entries. This substantiates the paragraph’s discussion of how input dimension affects table size. The scalar-SNR input, WITT implementation, and reconstruction measurements are explicitly presented as the citing manuscript’s own work. [CVF full paper, §§3.2–3.3](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2021/papers/Jo_Practical_Single-Image_Super-Resolution_Using_Look-Up_Table_CVPR_2021_paper.pdf#page=4)

**VERDICT — KEEP.** The reference and its single use are accurate.

IEEE Xplore returned a verification screen, and the DOI resolver and Crossref API pages were inaccessible through the web reader. The DOI was confirmed in Yonsei’s record.

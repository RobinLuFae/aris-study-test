# Citation audit — `krizhevsky2009learning`

**EXISTENCE YES.** The report's title page lists Alex Krizhevsky as sole author and dates the work April 8, 2009. [Original report](https://www.cs.toronto.edu/~kriz/learning-features-2009-TR.pdf)

**METADATA correct.** The author, title (*Learning Multiple Layers of Features from Tiny Images*), year, University of Toronto attribution, and report URL are correct. The official CIFAR page calls it a technical report and asks dataset users to cite it. Krizhevsky also lists it as his MSc thesis and identifies the Department of Computer Science, University of Toronto, in a subsequent paper. The primary sources give no DOI for the report, so the entry appropriately omits that field. [CIFAR reference](https://cave.cs.toronto.edu/kriz/cifar.html) · [Author's publications](https://cave.cs.toronto.edu/kriz/) · [Author's subsequent paper, reference 1](https://www.cs.utoronto.ca/~kriz/conv-cifar10-aug2010.pdf)

The official dataset page separately states both totals. The subsequent 45,000/5,000 subdivision and training settings are presented as the manuscript authors' protocol. [Report, §3.1](https://www.cs.toronto.edu/~kriz/learning-features-2009-TR.pdf) · [Official CIFAR-10 description](https://cave.cs.toronto.edu/kriz/cifar.html)
